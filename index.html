Archive of theSecond Continental War

Welcome to the website of my fictional world. I, KGK-16, have been working on the Archive for 5 years now and am glad to finally publish my work on this website.

The Archive and worldbuilding have definitely become my life's work, because whatever I get into, whatever people I meet, all of it finds its reflection here. When I look at the old versions of this world, I see my past self in them.

This world will never be finished; in fact, I even find it hard to make it structured. I am sure that the Archive will still undergo a huge number of changes.

Because I lack website-making skills, I made the code of this website with the help of Claude Code. This fact upsets me myself, and I would like to have a person who would help me with the technical side, to avoid using AI in such a personal project.
Nevertheless, I give all of you my word that not a single line of lore, plot or pixel of my drawings will ever be touched by AI - only the code of this website.

enjoy reading.
